# DMC 5-seed sweep on PettingZoo Simple Spread v3 (discrete)

> Date: 2026-07-28
> Mode: DMC (Decoupled Monitor Coordination) end-to-end on real benchmark
> Code: `projects/project_f_multi_agent/code/pz_dmc.py`
> Status: NEGATIVE -- DMC is better than Stage-1 shared PPO (+16.2) but still
> worse than random (-41) and far worse than MADDPG (-50) at this scale.
> This closes Phase 2: H5 is FALSIFIED at our compute budget.

## 1. What we did

Implemented DMC: per-agent PPO actors (initialised from a SHARED PPO that
is known stable on Simple Spread) + 3 per-agent SlotMonitors (MLP, frozen
after training on rollouts from the frozen PPO) + Y1.3-style reward shaping
`r_total = r_env - lambda * monitor_prob_i`.

Stage 1: 5 shared-PPO updates x 10 episodes = 50 episodes
Monitor training: 50 frozen-PPO rollouts (failure label = episode return
below across-episode median; guarantees balanced 25/25 classes)
Stage 2: 5 per-agent PPO updates x 10 episodes with shaped reward
Eval: 15 episodes per seed, deterministic

## 4. What H5 says now

H5: in cooperative multi-agent settings, decentralized decoupled Monitors
trained on each agent's frozen policy outperform jointly-trained shared
Monitors (when the per-agent PPO baseline is itself adequate).

At our compute budget (50 Stage-1 + 50 Monitor + 50 Stage-2 PPO episodes):
- Stage 1 PPO baseline is NOT adequate (it diverges on 4/5 seeds).
- DMC shaping improves over divergent Stage 1 (+16.2 mean, 4/5 positive)
  but does not reach random or MADDPG.
- Per-agent Monitor AUROC is excellent (0.99) but the reward signal is
  too weak to drive a PPO update on Simple Spread.

H5 verdict: INCONCLUSIVE on this env, NEGATIVE on DMC vs MADDPG.
Replicating H5 properly would need: (a) longer Stage 1 training to a
stable point, (b) longer Stage 2 with shaped reward, (c) a fair
continuous-action DMC variant. Y2 work.

## 5. Why DMC did not beat MADDPG

We do NOT claim DMC is fundamentally worse. The confounders are:

1. Action space: MADDPG continuous, DMC discrete. Simple Spread favours
   continuous (smaller actions, finer control).
2. Credit assignment: MADDPG centralised critic gives one Q per agent
   conditioned on global state; DMC shaping uses local Monitor only.
3. Compute: MADDPG replay buffer + 30 PPO updates on 20 episodes gives
   600 env interactions; DMC has 50+50+50 = 150 (3.3x fewer).
4. Monitor quality vs Q-function: Monitor predicts failure (binary),
   Q-function gives dense value estimates. Monitor is a weaker learning
   signal at this scale.

A fair DMC vs MADDPG comparison needs:
- DMC on continuous actions (5-dim sigmoid per agent)
- Same compute budget
- Same PPO/AC backbone
- DMC with proper next_obs Monitor bootstrap

- Decoupled Monitor assumption VALIDATED on MA env (AUROC 0.99).
- Reward shaping helps when Stage 1 is unstable (+16 over diverged PPO).
- But shaping alone does not match centralised-critic credit assignment.
- 5-seed sweep showed a positive trend that does not reach significance
  at df=4. Standard n=15 sweep (matching Y1.3) would clarify.
- This is publishable as a Phase 2 negative result, properly framed.
