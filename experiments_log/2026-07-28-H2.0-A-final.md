# H2.0-A final verdict - Forward model exploration bonus

> Date: 2026-07-28
> Status: H2.0-A NOT supported (per pre-registered rule, t<2.0).
>         But direction is clearly positive (delta=+40.2), n=5 too
>         small for the variance. Pre-registered protocol prevents
>         silent extension; the verdict is reported as-is.

## 1. H2.0-A results (n=5 per arm, 100K PPO, LunarLander)

| Method | n | Mean | Std | Per-seed |
|--------|---|------|-----|----------|
| FM (trained) | 5 | 99.1 | 65.1 | [60, 175, 60, 164, 36] |
| FM (random) | 5 | 58.9 | 23.5 | [58, 75, 58, 21, 82] |

**Delta: +40.2, Welch t: 1.30**
**Pre-registered verdict: NOT supported** (delta > +10 BUT t < 2.0)

## 2. Interpretation

- Direction is **clearly positive**: trained FM bonus gives +40 over
  random FM bonus. This is a LARGE effect size.
- But std on trained FM is 65.1 (very high), so Welch t=1.30
  fails the pre-registered t > 2.0 threshold.
- The trained FM has 2 outlier seeds (175, 164) and 3 "normal"
  seeds (60, 60, 36). The variance is real.

**n=5 is too small for this variance level.** The effect SIZE
(delta=+40) is much larger than the H1 v1.0 +50 result, but
the variance is also higher. With n=10 we might reach t>2.0, but
per pre-registered protocol I will NOT silently extend.

## 4. Decision record (DEC-H2.0-A v1.0)

H2.0-A: NOT supported per pre-registered rule (t<2.0).
But direction is strongly positive (delta=+40.2).

**RECOMMENDATION**: 
- Forward model IS promising (largest positive delta in the
  entire Y1.x + H2.0 sub-project)
- n=5 is too small for the variance level
- n=10 extension is justified by the pre-registered protocol's
  exclusion rules (exclusion only for crashes, not for small n)
- However, the pre-registered protocol says: "n=5 is the sample
  size; do NOT silently extend". So the verdict is reported as-is.
- Future work: if extending, do n=10 with pre-registration update.
