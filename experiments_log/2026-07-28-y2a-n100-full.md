## Statistical power analysis (final)

At observed effect size mean_diff=0.174, sd_diffs=1.19, n=100 gives
Cohen d_z = 0.147 (very small effect).

To reach p<0.05 with this effect size, we would need n~300 paired.
To reach p<0.01, n~600.

This means the effect, if real, is too small to be detected at typical
sample sizes (n=30-50) used in MA-RL papers. It would require a
dedicated study with n=300+ to confirm.
