## 1. NEGATIVE CONTROL RESULT (just completed)

| Method | n | Mean | Std |
|---|---|---|---|
| Y1.3 with REAL Monitor (lambda=0.5) | 15 | 80.1 | 45.9 |
| **Y1.3 with RANDOM Monitor (lambda=0.5)** | **5** | **58.2** | **51.7** |
| PPO baseline (no shaping) | 5 | 40.6 | 37.1 |

**Delta (real - random): 21.9 (n=5 vs n=15, NOT statistically significant)**

## 2. REVISED interpretation

The original Y1.3 v1.0 claim was:
  "Y1.3 (Monitor as PPO training-time regularizer) is the FIRST
   positive result. +50 over PPO baseline, t=6.76, p<0.001."

This was self-deceptive. The +50 came from **reward shaping during
PPO training**, NOT from the **Monitor signal specifically**.

When we add a NEGATIVE CONTROL (Y1.3 with RANDOM monitor signal in
place of the trained Monitor), the effect is:
  - Random monitor: +17.6 over PPO baseline (40.6 -> 58.2)
  - Real monitor:    +39.5 over PPO baseline (40.6 -> 80.1)
  - Real over random: +21.9 (n=5 vs n=15, n.s.)

The +17.6 from random shaping is the dominant effect. This means
**the Y1.3 procedure (any reward shaping during PPO) helps PPO
on LunarLander**, but the **specific Monitor signal adds only
marginal extra value** that is NOT distinguishable from chance
with the current sample size.

## 3. What this means

### 3.1 What Y1.3 ACTUALLY shows
- Reward shaping during PPO training (with ANY signal source) helps
  PPO on LunarLander-v3 by roughly +18 to +40 mean reward
- The signal source (trained Monitor vs random uniform) does NOT
  significantly affect the outcome with n=15
- The mechanism is "regularization" or "exploration perturbation",
  not "Monitor signal guides policy"

### 3.2 What Y1.3 does NOT show
- That the Monitor signal is useful (it adds < +22 over random)
- That Y1.3 is specifically due to the decoupled Monitor architecture
- That this is a generalizable finding (only 1 env tested, only 1
  intervention variant tested)

## 6. Lessons for future work

1. **Always run a negative control BEFORE announcing a result.**
   The P0 checklist in NO_SELF_DECEPTION.md is mandatory.
2. **A "real signal" vs "no signal" comparison is insufficient.**
   The right comparison is "real signal" vs "random signal",
   because the act of perturbing PPO reward may itself be useful
   (regularization / exploration).
3. **A single t-statistic against a convenient baseline is suspicious.**
   t=6.76 against PPO-only does not prove the Monitor is the
   cause. It only proves the Y1.3 procedure is better than PPO-only.
4. **Twitter/Discord drafts must include the negative control**
   even if the result is "we got lucky and it works for unclear
   reasons." Overclaiming kills credibility.

## 7. Open follow-up

Run the **inverse-monitor control** (Y1.3 with `1 - real_monitor_prob`
as the shaping signal) on 5 seeds. If this gives < +18 mean: the
real Monitor signal is at least directionally informative. If this
gives +50 mean: the Monitor signal is not informative, only the
shaping procedure is. This is the next P0 control.
