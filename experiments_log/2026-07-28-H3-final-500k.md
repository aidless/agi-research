# H3 final verdict - 500K PPO, n=5 per arm (LunarLander)

> Date: 2026-07-28
> Status: H3 NOT supported. 5x more PPO training REVERSES the
>         sign of the real-vs-random delta. Y1.3 with real Monitor
>         is actively WORSE than Y1.3 with random Monitor at 500K.
> This is a meaningful negative result for the Y1.3 sub-project.

## 1. H3 final results (n=5 per arm, 500K PPO on LunarLander)

| Method | n | Mean | Std | Per-seed |
|--------|---|------|-----|----------|
| Y1.3 (real, 500K) | 5 | 170.7 | 49.9 | [seed 0-4] |
| Y1.3 (random, 500K) | 5 | **223.9** | 23.0 | [seed 0-4] |

**Delta (real - random): -53.1, Welch t = -2.16**
**Pre-registered verdict: H3 NOT supported** (delta < +10 OR t < 2.0)

## 2. Comparison to H1 (100K PPO)

| Method | 100K PPO (H1) | 500K PPO (H3) |
|--------|----------------|----------------|
| Y1.3 (real) | 80.1 (n=15) | 170.7 (n=5) |
| Y1.3 (random) | 66.5 (n=10) | **223.9 (n=5)** |
| Delta (real - random) | +13.6 (NOT sig) | **-53.1 (t=-2.16)** |
| Monitor status: | not validated | **actively hurts** |

**5x more PPO:**
- Real Monitor reward shape went from 80 to 171 (+91, PPO converged)
- Random Monitor reward shape went from 66.5 to **223.9 (+157.4)**
- Random is now BETTER than real by 53 points

## 3. Interpretation: Y1.3 is "Monitor noise as PPO noise"

The result suggests that with more PPO training, the Monitor's
reward perturbation becomes a **distraction** rather than a help.
The Monitor provides a learned signal correlated with failure, but
at 500K steps PPO has its own (better) gradient signal from the
real reward. The Monitor perturbation adds noise without
proportionate information.

This is consistent with prior findings:
  - PPO at 100K has high variance; Monitor perturbation acts as
    a useful regularizer
  - PPO at 500K has lower variance; Monitor perturbation is now
    a pure noise injection that hurts convergence

## 4. Pre-registered verdict (DEC-H3 v1.0)

H3: "Real Monitor is informative above random at 500K PPO"
Result: Real - Random = -53.1, t=-2.16. H3 NOT supported.
  - delta is negative (Real < Random), not positive
  - t = -2.16 is borderline significant in magnitude but in the
    wrong direction (Random > Real)
  - Per pre-registered decision rule (delta > +10 AND t > 2.0):
    H3 fails decisively

**FINAL verdict of Y1.3 sub-project (v1.5):**
  - At 100K PPO: Y1.3 ~ PPO (no help, mixed evidence)
  - At 500K PPO: Y1.3 < PPO (active interference)
  - Cross-env: Y1.3 ~ PPO on Acrobot (no help)
  - Across 3 conditions (2 PPO budgets + 1 env), Y1.3 with trained
    Monitor never significantly HELPS PPO
  - At 500K PPO, Y1.3 with trained Monitor is WORSE than
    Y1.3 with random Monitor (t=-2.16)

The Y1.3 sub-project should be CLOSED. The Monitor architecture
provides useful real-time failure prediction (Sections 4.6-4.8,
AUROC 0.99) but does NOT transfer to policy improvement via
reward shaping at any PPO budget we tested (100K or 500K).

## 5. What to do next

The Y1.3 sub-project is now well-characterized. Three independent
tests (H1 100K + H2 prelim Acrobot + H3 500K) all show Y1.3
does not help PPO. Further iteration on the same intervention
(unrealistic Monitor architecture, any lambda, any PPO budget)
is unlikely to flip the verdict.

Recommended next direction: NEW intervention. Options:
  - Different Monitor use case (e.g., Monitor as EXPLORATION signal,
    not reward perturbation)
  - Monitor for ROLLOUT FILTERING (drop rollouts with high Monitor
    score, instead of using Monitor for reward shaping)
  - Imitation learning (Y1.4 idea) with the Monitor as a quality
    signal for expert demonstrations
  - New domain (Procgen, more complex envs) where Monitor's
    failure prediction might be more decisive

I do NOT recommend extending Y1.3 further. The 3 tests are
sufficient evidence that the intervention does not work as
currently designed.

## 6. Lessons for future work

1. **Pre-registration works.** H1, H2, H3 all had clear pre-registered
   decision rules. The verdicts were unambiguous.
2. **n=5 per arm is enough to detect large effects.** H3 caught a
   -53 effect with t=-2.16 at n=5. Smaller effects (like H1's
   +13.6) need n=10+.
3. **Pilot results generalize poorly.** v1.0's n=5 "POSITIVE +50"
   result was misleading. Always do a pre-registered replication
   before claiming.
4. **The Monitor architecture is real but its USE is the problem.**
   The Monitor predicts failure well (AUROC 0.99) but using it
   for reward shaping is the wrong use case. Future work should
   consider other uses.
