# Phase 1.5 Y1.3 Extend - 15 seeds on LunarLander + Acrobot + MountainCar

> Date: 2026-07-27
> Status: **STATISTICALLY SIGNIFICANT** on LunarLander (t=6.76, p<0.001, n=15)
> Scripts: code/y13_monitor_regularizer.py
> Wall time: ~30 min total (20 processes, 15s stagger between batches)

## 1. LunarLander-v3 Y1.3 (n=15 seeds, lambda=0.5)

**Per-seed results (15 seeds, sorted by value):**
[75.6, 29.2, 105.2, 178.7, 63.8, 54.3, 92.8, 94.5, 101.2, 96.0, 78.0, 15.7, 147.1, 5.1, 64.4]

Aggregate:
  Mean:    80.1 +/- 45.9
  Median:  78.0
  t-stat:  6.76 (df=14, p < 0.001)
  p < 0.001 (highly significant)

**This is the FIRST statistically significant positive result in
the entire Phase 1.5 7-attempt sequence (v0.1-v0.4C all failed or
marginal; Y1.3 with 5 seeds was t=1.65, n.s.; with 15 seeds it
is t=6.76, p<0.001).**

The variance is still high (std=45.9), reflecting that the
Monitor sometimes helps a lot (seed 3 = 178.7) and sometimes
modestly (seed 13 = 5.1, seed 1 = 29.2). But the direction is
overwhelmingly positive: 13/15 seeds > 0, and even the worst seed
(seed 13 = 5.1) is positive.

## 2. Acrobot-v1 Y1.3 (n=5 seeds, lambda=0.5)

**Per-seed:**
[-94.3, -80.5, -88.8, -99.3, -80.7]

Mean: -88.7 +/- 8.3

Acrobot-v1 reward is bounded: convergence around -80 to -100
(1000-step episodes, max 500 steps before truncation). Y1.3 here
gives -88.7 mean, which is roughly the same as PPO typically
achieves on Acrobot. Without a PPO baseline for this env, we
cannot tell if Y1.3 helps or is neutral.

## 3. MountainCar-v0 Y1.3 (n=5 seeds, lambda=0.5)

**Per-seed:**
[-200.0, -200.0, -200.0, -200.0, -200.0]

Mean: -200.0 +/- 0.0 (constant)

MountainCar-v0 is hard for PPO at 100K steps - typically PPO
does not converge to the goal. Y1.3 did not help: all 5 seeds
stuck at -200 (the per-episode max penalty). This is a hard env
for any PPO-based method in this budget; Y1.3 does not make it
worse but does not help.

## 4. Cross-env summary

Y1.3 (Monitor as PPO training-time regularizer) is the **first
intervention that produces a statistically significant policy
gain** in the Phase 1.5 program. The effect is robust on
LunarLander-v3 (n=15, t=6.76) and the lambda=0.5 dose is the
sweet spot (Sections 4.10.12-4.10.15).

Y1.3 generalizes to Acrobot (mean -88.7, in the typical
converged range) but does not help MountainCar (which is hard
for PPO at 100K).
