## 3. Interpretation

The Monitor architecture is real (AUROC 0.99) but its SIGNAL does
not improve PPO in any tested intervention:
  - Reward shaping (penalize Monitor_prob): not useful
  - Exploration bonus (reward Monitor_prob): not useful
  - Different PPO budgets (100K and 500K): not useful
  - Different envs (LunarLander, Acrobot): not useful

This is strong evidence that the Monitor is NOT useful for ONLINE
POLICY IMPROVEMENT. The Monitor is good for OFFLINE analysis
(Section 4.6-4.8, prediction level) but does not transfer to
action-level gains in PPO.

**RECOMMENDATION**: STOP using the Monitor for online policy

fails) but should not be in the PPO training loop.

## 6. Lessons for future work

1. **Pre-registration works.** Each H test had a clear
   pre-registered decision rule. The verdicts are unambiguous.
2. **A good architecture does not mean a good use case.** The
   Monitor predicts failure well (AUROC 0.99) but using it for
   online PPO training does not help. Future work should
   distinguish the model from the use case.
3. **Negative results are publishable.** 4 independent tests
   showing no benefit is a meaningful research finding. This
   rules out a class of interventions for future work.
4. **Self-deception compounds but pre-registration prevents it.**
   Y1.3 v1.0 overclaimed at n=5. Y1.3 v1.3 with n=15 per arm
   honestly reported H1 NOT supported. Y1.4 with pre-registration
   avoided the v1.0 overclaim from the start.
