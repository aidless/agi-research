## Interpretation

- ALL p30 thresholds = 0.0 even at 256K steps. This is worse than I expected.
- 8531 episodes collected across 4 games.
- The 30th percentile of training returns is 0 = 30%+ of episodes returned 0
  reward throughout training.
- Mean returns are still low for bigfish (1.0), dodgeball (1.2) -- these are
  games where Procgen easy distribution may not yield much signal at this PPO scale.

## Implication for Project A paper v1

- Phase 1 (PPO baseline) needs *vastly* more compute (1M+ steps / game) to
  produce diverse enough failure labels for Phase 2 Monitor to detect.
- Or: relax the failure definition (e.g. use length-based failure, not reward-
  based).
- Or: change the experiment to a smaller environment where PPO learns faster.
- The pipeline-level claim (frozen Monitor runs end-to-end, no infrastructure
  issues) is intact. The empirical H1 directional claim is delayed until
  Phase 1 yields enough variance.
