## 3. Synthesis

### 3.1 The pattern across 6 experiments

The progression of the delta std reveals the underlying issue:
- v0.1 (n=5 eval): std=67 (just noise)
- v0.2 (n=50 eval, 200 train): std=209 (calibration overfits)
- v0.4A (n=50 eval, 1000 train): **std=17** (calibration honest, neutral)

The std dropped from 209 to 17 when we increased train from 200 to 1000.
This is the 5x data requirement: **<500 train episodes is too few**;
**>=1000 is sufficient** to avoid val-set overfit.

### 3.2 Why none of the experiments shows significant help

Across all 6 experiments, the **best** mean delta is +21.5 (v0.1, n=5 eval)
which is not significant. With n=50 eval (3x more honest), the best is
v0.4A at -1.8 (also not significant). Even with 5x more training data,
**gating does not provide a reliable policy gain**.

This suggests:
1. The Monitor's prediction signal is real (AUROC 0.789-0.99) but does
   not directly translate to action value in the current setup.
2. The PPO policy on LunarLander-v3 is already near-optimal for
   PPO-based methods; gating adds noise more than signal.
3. With 1000 train episodes, the calibration is honest but the action
   selection (Q-BoN, imitation, safe action) is still too crude to
   convert Monitor signal into consistent policy gain.

### 3.3 What v0.4A teaches us about data requirements

v0.2 (200 train) -> catastrophic: val_auroc=1.0 overfits, cal_threshold=~0,
gating destroys policy.
v0.4A (1000 train) -> neutral: val_auroc=0.97, cal_threshold=0.1-0.65,
gating matches PPO.

The transition is sharp: 5x more data turns a -158 negative into a -2
neutral. The "1000 train episodes" is the **minimum useful data size**
for calibrated gating on LunarLander-v3.

## 4. Decision record (DEC-0011 v0.4 final)

> **DEC-0011 v0.4 status: HALT the online-gating sub-project.**
>
> Across 6 independent experiments (v0.1, v0.2, v0.3, v0.4A, v0.4B,
> v0.4C) on 2 environments (LunarLander-v3, CartPole-v1) with 3 action-
> selection strategies (Q-BoN, safe action, behavior cloning) and
> varying data sizes (200, 1000 train episodes):
>
> - 1 experiment (v0.1) is mixed and likely noise
> - 4 experiments (v0.2, v0.3, v0.4B, v0.4C) are NEGATIVE
> - 1 experiment (v0.4A) is NEUTRAL (t=-0.25, 3/5 pos)
> - **0 experiments show statistically significant HELP**
>
> The decoupling contribution is **conceptual and prediction-level**
> (5-seed AUROC delta=0.793 on LunarLander). It does NOT extend to
> policy-action level with current techniques and data scales.
>
> **Recommended next direction**: stop the gating sub-project.
> Move to Y1 work: model-based planning, expert demonstration
> imitation, or different environment.
