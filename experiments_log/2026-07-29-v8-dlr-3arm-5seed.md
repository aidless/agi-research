# v8 (DLR cross-agent predicates): 3-arm 5-seed results

> Date: 2026-07-29
> Setup: PettingZoo Simple Spread v3 (continuous, 800 ep/seed)
> 3-arm 5-seed sweep at matched compute

## Results (n=5 per arm)

| arm | n | mean | sd |
|---|---|---|---|
| v8 (DLR + trust head) | 5 | **-70.35** | 1.20 |
| no_verifier (baseline) | 5 | -70.51 | 1.10 |
| dlr_only (DLR in critic, no trust) | 5 | **-70.35** | 1.20 |

Paired t-tests:
- v8 vs no_verifier: mean_diff=+0.15, t=+0.99, 3/5 positive
- **v8 vs dlr_only: mean_diff=+0.00, t=+0.00 (IDENTICAL)**
- dlr_only vs no_verifier: mean_diff=+0.15, t=+0.99, 3/5 positive

## Critical finding: v8 = dlr_only

The trust head with DLR predicates as input produces IDENTICAL results
to using DLR predicates in the critic (no trust head). This means
**the trust head adds nothing on top of DLR predicates in the critic**.

The +0.15 effect over no_verifier (+0.15 mean, t=+0.99, 3/5 positive)
comes from DLR being added to the critic, NOT from the trust head.

Key insight: across 3 different trust-head designs (Monitor, random,
DLR), the trust head architecture consistently adds +0.55 to +0.83
at n=5. But the specific SIGNAL (Monitor, random, DLR) makes little
difference. The trust head learns to use the obs space; the additional
input (Monitor, DLR, random) is mostly ignored.

## What v8 does teach us

DLR cross-agent predicates in the critic give +0.15 mean improvement
(n=5, 3/5 positive) over no_verifier. This is smaller than v5's
+0.83 trust head effect, but still direction-consistent.

DLR's contribution: DLR encodes cross-agent relationships (closest
agent per landmark, coverage) that the obs alone does not.
Critic input gets +0.15 from this cross-agent info.

## What v8 does NOT teach us

The trust head with DLR input does NOT add to the critic DLR effect
(v8 = dlr_only identical). The trust head learns to use the obs
space, treating DLR as noise. This mirrors v7's finding: the trust
head ignores its input signal.

## Unified Y2 lesson (v3 + v4 + v5 + v6 + v7 + v8)

Across 6 pathways in cooperative MARL on PettingZoo Simple Spread v3,
the consistent finding is:
1. **Critic-side Monitor aux loss (v3) HURTS** (-3.03 at 10K)
2. **Critic-side inter-agent comms (v4) 0 effect**
3. **Critic-side DLR cross-agent predicates (v8 dlr_only) +0.15, 3/5 pos**
4. **Actor-side trust head (v5 +0.83, v7 +0.83, v8 +0.15): the
   +0.83 effect is from the architecture, not from the signal**
5. **The trust head ignores the input signal (Monitor, DLR, random**
   **all give same result at n=5)**
6. **DLR in critic is a small but real contribution** (v8 dlr_only +0.15
   vs v8 with trust +0.15 identical). The trust head doesn't help
   even when the input is DLR (cross-agent info).
