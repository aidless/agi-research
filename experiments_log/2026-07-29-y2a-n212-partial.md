## Statistical power analysis (FINAL)

At observed effect size mean_diff=0.055, sd_diffs=0.84, n=212 gives
Cohen d_z = 0.065 (extremely small effect). To reach p<0.05 would need
n~2200 paired samples. To reach p<0.01, n~3700. This is unrealistic
for typical MA-RL papers.

**Honest interpretation**: the effect, if real, is so small (<1% of
baseline) that it is not practically meaningful even if statistically
significant at very large n. The Monitor as MA verifier IS direction-
consistent (107/212 = 50.5% > 50%) but the magnitude is too small to
matter in practice.
