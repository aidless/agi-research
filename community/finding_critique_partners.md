# Finding critique partners - tactical playbook

> Tactics for the hardest part of being independent: getting a real
> person to read your draft. Target: 2 critique partners by M6.

---

## Why this is the hardest problem

Independents have no institutional inbox. 90% of cold emails to PIs
are ignored. 99% of Discord / Reddit posts get 0 substantive replies.
We need a strategy that uses the small surface area we have well.

## What critique partners actually do

A critique partner is NOT a mentor. They are NOT collaborator. They
are a peer who:
- reads one of our drafts in a week
- writes 1 page of feedback
- is happy to be cited in the acknowledgements

Time cost on their side: ~4 hours per paper. That is real. They will
only spend it if the paper is interesting, the ask is concrete, and
the reciprocation is real.

## Tactics ranked by efficiency

### 1. arXiv comment culture (highest signal)
- Post a question or short comment on the public arXiv discussion
  page of a paper we are reading.
- The paper authors sometimes reply. Reply back with thoughtful follow-up.
- Within 2-3 rounds you have a conversation that may lead to them
  reading your draft.

### 2. Twitter DM with concrete question (high)
- Find an author of a paper we cite.
- Send a DM, NOT a generic "love your work" line. Include:
  - one specific question about their paper
  - a sentence about what we are working on
  - "happy to reciprocate when you ship your next paper"
- 30% reply rate if question is sharp.

### 3. Email to author with one-paragraph pitch (medium)
- Send to authors of recent papers we cite (last 6 months).
- Subject line is critical: include your finding, not a generic request.
- Attach a 2-page summary, NOT a 30-page draft.
- 10-20% reply rate.

### 4. Discord channels (medium-low)
- ML Collective, World Models Discord, AI Safety Discord.
- Be active for 1-2 weeks BEFORE asking for anything.
- Then ask a specific question that requires reading our draft.
- Higher reciprocal success if you help others first.

### 5. Reddit r/MachineLearning (low but visible)
- "Sunday Showcase" / "Show your work" posts get some eyes.
- 1-2 critique partners per quarter from this channel is realistic.

### 6. Workshop CFP (medium)
- Submit to workshop before submitting to main conference.
- Workshop reviewers often reciprocate critique on your draft.

## Reciprocity is the unlock

Whatever tactic we use, the ask must be symmetric. We must offer:
- an acknowledgement line
- a public endorsement when they ship
- a 1-hour review of their next paper

Most "cold" asks fail because the asker only wants to take.

## Targets by M6 (2 critique partners minimum)

To reach this, we must:
- make 3 Twitter DM contacts per week (12 total)
- reply to 1 arXiv comment per week
- post 1 substantive Discord / Reddit comment per week
- send 1 cold email per week

If 30% reply, we get 4-5 conversations. Of those, 2 become ongoing
critique partners.
