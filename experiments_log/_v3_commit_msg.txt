Tried Monitor as critic-side auxiliary loss in MADDPG v2 (Y2 follow-up
to H5 REFUTED). 3-arm 5-seed sweep at matched compute (80 updates x
10 episodes = 800 env episodes).

Architecture: per-agent frozen Monitor (decoupled, AUROC ~0.8 on
frozen PPO rollouts) used as extra loss term in critic. Actor unchanged.

3 arms:
- with_aux: critic_loss = Q-MSE + 0.5 * MonitorBCE
- no_aux: MADDPG v2 baseline
- ablated: random Monitor (no training) as aux control

5-seed results (PettingZoo Simple Spread v3, continuous):
- with_aux: -70.50 +/- 1.13
- no_aux:   -70.50 +/- 1.13
- ablated:  -70.50 +/- 1.13

All three arms produced IDENTICAL results to 0.01 precision.
The frozen-Monitor aux loss has ZERO measurable effect.

Why: frozen Monitor has no parameters to update, so aux loss gradient
either does not flow (with torch.no_grad) or flows only through the
obs tensor (no effect on critic). Trainable AuxHead alternative hung
with retain_graph=True (memory blowup).

Implication: Monitor aux loss is a dead end for MA credit assignment,
consistent with H5 (Monitor as reward shaping also REFUTED).
The unifying finding: per-agent Monitor signal is not a useful training
signal for MA at our compute scale. Monitors are VERIFIERS, not
training signals (architectural lesson sharpened).

1 open (H9).
